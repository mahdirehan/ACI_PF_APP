Asset Condition Index (ACI) 28 tests

The Asset Condition Index (ACI) measures the overall condition of an asset on a scale of 0-100. It combines functional and appearance scores based on the asset type and detected defects.

Condition Factor (CF) 20 tests

The Condition Factor (CF) converts ACI into a priority boost value. Assets in poor condition get higher CF values (more urgent), while assets in good condition get CF=0 (no urgency).

Formula

=IF(ACI >= 80, 0, (80 - ACI) / 80 * 100)

Functional Life Factor (FLF) 26 tests

The Functional Life Factor (FLF) estimates how much of an asset's useful life has been consumed based on its current condition. Each asset type has a unique deterioration curve derived from historical data.

Formula

ConsumedLife = (ACI - intercept) / slope
FLF = IF(ACI=0, 100, IF(ACI>=80, 0, MAX(0, MIN(100, (ConsumedLife/TotalLife)*100))))

Risk Factor (RF) 22 tests

The Risk Factor (RF) represents the inherent risk/importance of an asset type, independent of its current condition. Critical infrastructure like traffic signals has high RF, while decorative elements like benches have low RF.

Formula

RF = 10 + ((Score - MIN) / (MAX - MIN)) * (100 - 10)

Priority Factor (PF) 19 tests

The Priority Factor (PF) is the final aggregated score that combines condition (CF), risk (RF), and life consumption (FLF) to determine which assets should be prioritized for maintenance or replacement.

Formula

PF = CF × 0.6 + RF × 0.2 + FLF × 0.2

Default weights: CF=60%, RF=20%, FLF=20%
